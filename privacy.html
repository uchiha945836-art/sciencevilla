Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Welcome to Science Villa ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website (regardless of where you visit it from) and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

When you visit or use Science Villa, we may collect several types of information from and about users, including:

  • Personal Identification Data: Information you voluntarily provide when filling out forms on our platform (such as your name, institutional affiliation, and email address).

  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

4. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way, altered, or disclosed. Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized personnel who have a business need to know.
