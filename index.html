Chemistry Spotlight: Atomic Structure

An atom is the basic building block of matter. It consists of a dense central region called the nucleus surrounded by an electron cloud.

Protons (+)

Positively charged particles located in the nucleus. The number of protons defines the atomic number and identifies the element.

Neutrons (0)

Uncharged (neutral) particles in the nucleus that add mass and stability, holding protons together against electromagnetic repulsion.

Electrons (-)

Negatively charged particles orbiting the nucleus in energy levels (shells). They govern chemical bonding and reactivity.

Electron Shells

Electrons fill discrete energy levels: up to 2 in the 1st shell (K-shell), 8 in the 2nd shell (L-shell), and 8 or 18 in higher shells.
